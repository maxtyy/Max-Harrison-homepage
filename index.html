QuantForge

QUANTITATIVE FINANCE

A Python platform for quantitative research, backtesting, and local paper trading. Explore systematic strategies through an interactive dashboard, with explicit trading costs and risk controls.

  • Moving average trend, time-series momentum, and mean reversion strategies.
  • Next-open backtesting with commissions, slippage, and performance reports.
  • Position limits, volatility scaling, and drawdown controls.
  • Persistent paper accounts with cash checks and duplicate-order protection.
  • Python
  • pandas
  • FastAPI
  • SQLite
  • GitHub Actions
